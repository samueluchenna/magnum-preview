Questions

Before you book

How do I get a price?

Use 'Build your clean' at the top of this page, or send a WhatsApp message to 0905 294 2567 with the space, the area and the date. Magnum replies with a price before any work starts.

Will cleaning disrupt my office?

No. Daily deep cleans or weekly touch-ups are planned around your operational hours, so your team can keep working.

Are your products safe for children and staff?

We use premium, non-toxic products that protect your family, your team and the environment.

Who will be in my home or office?

Our cleaning professionals are fully vetted, bonded and trained to respect your privacy and security.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Victoria Island and work across Lagos, including Lekki and Ikeja. Tell us your area when you ask for a price.

Do you handle fumigation and soak-away evacuation too?

Yes. Alongside cleaning, Magnum does fumigation, soak-away evacuation and pressure washing, so one call covers the whole job.
