About Magnum

A Lagos cleaning, fumigation and sewage evacuation company, based on Ademola Adetokunbo Street, Victoria Island.

Who we are

Clean. Care. Confidence.

Magnum Cleaning Services looks after homes, short-lets, offices, estates, shops and hospitals across Lagos. Alongside everyday cleaning, we handle the heavy jobs too: post-construction cleaning, fumigation, soak-away evacuation and pressure washing.

Tailored schedulesDaily deep cleans or weekly touch-ups, planned around your operational hours, with no disruptions.
Eco-friendly and safePremium, non-toxic products that protect your team and the environment.
Reliable and insuredOur cleaning professionals are fully vetted, bonded and trained to respect your privacy and security.
